Getting started

After installation, the Setup guide opens: you pick your main Alza, it checks your sign-in and prices incl. VAT in one click (each item says what's done and what to do) and walks you through watching (including My purchases), category listings and notifications. Run it again from the settings ( Setup guide, at the bottom of the left panel); change the main Alza in the settings on the General tab.

After an update with a new option, the What's new page opens by itself: a short list of what's new and the new options in green with a New label – you can switch them on or off right there. After other updates, a green What's new badge shows in the extension window. Open the news any time with the What's new link in the settings (bottom left) or the green badge in the extension window.

  1. Log in to Alza. Prices are saved only when you are logged in – Alza shows different prices to different accounts (AlzaPlus+, benefits, B2B).
  2. Show prices including VAT. With "show prices without VAT" turned on, prices are not saved so they don't get mixed up.
  3. Open a shopping list (My account → Shopping lists). The extension loads every price of each item and starts keeping a history.
  4. Turn on watching – the extension then checks the list regularly (every hour by default) even with Alza closed and lets you know when something gets cheaper.

History is kept for products in shopping lists, for products you watch and, with the “Save prices while browsing” option (on by default), also for products you see while browsing Alza – in category listings, search results, product pages, carousels and on the home page. The longer you use the extension, the more it can tell you – the price rating and the strikethrough-price check need at least 14 days of history.

Shopping list

  • All prices of an item – besides the regular price also the sale price, price with a discount code, AlzaPlus+ price, cashback… sorted from the lowest.
  • Hovering over a price shows a card: the price name (Benefit, Discount code, Black Friday…), the difference to higher prices, the minimum and maximum, and for a discount code the code with a Copy button.
  • Click a price or the availability to open the product's price history.
  • Arrows next to the price: ▼ cheaper than usual right now, ▲ more expensive than usual (hover for details). = you set a target price, green = it has been reached.
  • Changes since last time – items that got cheaper or more expensive since the last load show the previous price and the difference.
  • Several pieces – prices stay per piece, with a "3 pcs total" column and the total to their left (including volume discounts). Once a volume discount tier applies, the price per piece for that quantity is shown in green before the prices – hover for the tier table and your savings. Nothing in the row moves.
  • Conditional price and the cart – when the lowest price applies only with a discount code, with AlzaPlus+ or after cashback / trade-in, the extension warns you before adding the item to the cart from the list You can still add it. With a discount code, the warning shows the code with a Copy button and an Add with code button that puts the item in the cart and applies the code right away (like “Buy with code” on the product page).
  • Code check in the cart – when the cart contains an item for which the extension knows a discount code from the product page and the discount is missing, it warns you after clicking “Continue”. Apply the code with one click, or continue without it.
  • Ratings in the cart – under each cart item there is a row with the same information as in the list: ▼▲ against the usual price, worth waiting, suspicious discount (a suspicious discount and a crossed-out price in one label), a cheaper unpacked / used piece. With a volume discount the row has a “+1 pc → lower price per piece” button that adds the piece right away. Hover for details.
  • You have it – for goods you have already bought (My purchases, or a purchase in the last 14 days) the cart shows “You have it · bought …”; hover for the price and number of pieces. So you do not buy the same thing twice by mistake.
  • Summary above the cart – how many items are above the usual price (and by how much in total), how many below it, where it is worth waiting and where the discount is suspicious. With a monthly budget set, also how much you spent this month, how much it will be with the cart and how much is left.

Bar above the list

  • Summary: number of items, what you pay (by quantity, including volume discounts), cashback shown separately, saving against list prices, and how many items got cheaper / more expensive.
  • Filters: cheaper since last time, on sale, below usual price, worth waiting, volume discount, cheaper in another condition, in stock, target reached, suspicious discount. Hover a price to see the volume discount steps. The number in brackets shows how many items match.
  • Sorting: cheapest, cheapest per piece, most expensive, biggest discount, furthest below usual price, biggest drop since last time.

Filters and sorting only hide and reorder items – they don't change the list on Alza itself.

Product page

On a product page, the left column (below the gallery) has a Price history card:

  • Current – the lowest price you can pay, with a rating against the usual price below it.
  • Per piece – the real price per piece for multipacks (even without history).
  • Usual price, lowest in 30 days, all-time low and a small chart – click the chart to open the full history.
  • Price comparison icons (Heureka, Zboží.cz on alza.cz; Heureka.sk; Árukereső) – search for the product by name in a new tab. The icons are loaded directly from the comparison site; if they fail to load, the site's name is shown instead.
  • Copy offer (icon bottom right) – copies the name, price, price with a code or AlzaPlus+, usual price, lowest price in 30 days and the link to the clipboard. Handy for a chat or social media.
  • Volume discount – the table of “buy more, pay less” tiers (price per piece at 1, 2, 3… pieces) is shown right away in a bubble above the cart button; Alza shows it only once the product is in the cart. When prices aren't being saved (e.g. not signed in), the card says why.
  • Running cost – for fridges, freezers, washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, TVs and monitors, the estimated electricity cost, e.g. “Running cost, 10 years: 6 570 Kč · with the purchase price 26 560 Kč” (see Real price per piece).
  • You bought it on… – for goods you already own, the purchase date and price, the warranty end and a link to the order (with My purchases on).
  • Watch in background – the product is checked regularly even if it is not in any list.
  • Target price – see Target price.
  • Note (icon bottom right) – your own note on the product (for example “wait for Black Friday”); it shows in the card and in category listings above the product name.
  • Reviews inside out (icon bottom right) – loads up to 300 reviews from Alza and shows what bothers people (words that repeat in the cons; click a word for an example), how the rating of the last 3 months differs from before and the most critical reviews from verified purchases.

Price history

Open the history dialog by clicking a price in a list or the "full history and chart" button on a product page.

  • Tiles at the top: all-time low, highest selling and list price, lowest price in 30 days, usual price and tracked since. The time of the last check is in the header next to the close button.
  • Chart: each price type has its own colour, the coloured band at the bottom is availability. Horizontal lines mark the minimum, maximum and target price. Hover to see prices for that period. Zoom into a crowded stretch by dragging across the chart, with Ctrl + wheel (pinch on a touchpad) or with the 24 h / 7 days / 30 days buttons above it; Shift + wheel pans, double-click or “All” shows the whole period. A grey hatched band labelled “not tracked” marks a time when the extension did not see the price – the line is interrupted there.
  • Table: every change of prices and availability with from–to times.

Is the price really good?

Usual price and rating

The usual price is the price the product sold at for the longest time in the last 180 days – a short sale doesn't pull it down. The current price is compared with it:

  • ▼ Lowest in X days / X % below the usual price – a good time to buy.
  • At the usual price now – within 3 %.
  • ▲ X % above the usual price – worth waiting.

Hover to also see how many times the product was cheaper than usual and how long it lasted.

Sale rhythm – worth waiting?

When the product had at least 3 sales in the last 180 days, the extension works out how often, how long and how deep its sales are (e.g. "On sale roughly once every 6 weeks, usually −15 % for 7 days") and when the next one can be expected. If the next sale is due within 3 weeks, it shows worth waiting – next to the price and as a filter in lists, in blue in the history card on the product page. Irregular sales are not predicted.

This time last year

When a product's history goes back more than a year, the card on the product page shows how the price moved last year in the coming month – for example “Last year it dropped to 1 990 Kč in the following month (25/11)” before Black Friday. In the cart the item then shows “Last year a month later …” when it was noticeably cheaper last year.

Browsing memory in listings

In category listings and search, above the product name you see “viewed 3/11” for products whose page you have already opened (with saving prices while browsing on), and your note – you write it on the product page with the Note button in the extension card. Hover a tile to Hide the product or the whole brand (taken from Alza's Brand filter, otherwise from the start of the name). Above the listing you then see “Hidden in this listing: N” with a Show hidden button – hidden products show semi-transparent and Show again brings them back. Everything you have hidden is listed on the extension page under My data → Hidden in listings. Notes and hidden products are in the backup.

The same product in another condition

Alza sells some products unpacked or like new too (each condition is a separate product). When another condition is cheaper than the lowest price you pay for this item (including AlzaPlus+ and benefit), you see it in the card on the product page and when hovering the price in a list (e.g. “Cheaper in another condition: Like new 6 459 Kč (231 Kč less)”); lists also have a Cheaper in another condition filter.

Discount filter in categories

In the left filter panel (the AllyZap block at the top), choose Discount at least X % or tick Coupon only (a price with AlzaPlus+ or a discount code) or Volume discount (“buy more, pay less”). The extension loads the whole listing (up to 500 products) and hides products that don't match. The discount is calculated from the lowest price you pay – including AlzaPlus+ and discount codes. Products with history are compared with the usual price, so a crossed-out price that hasn't been charged for a long time can't inflate the discount; others with Alza's crossed-out price. A larger listing (categories can have tens of thousands of products) isn't loaded automatically – the filter checks the loaded products and “Load 480 more” adds more. To pick from all of them, narrow the listing with Alza's filters (subcategory, price, brand) – once it has up to 500 products, it loads whole. For the “at least X %” filter in a large listing, use Alza's discount sort tab (“Podle slevy” on Alza.cz): the most discounted products come first, so the first loaded batches already contain those with the highest discount (Alza calculates it from the crossed-out price, so the order may not exactly match the extension's discount). You can turn it off in the settings (Category listings → Discount filter).

Suspicious discount

  • Cheaper within 30 days: the product is "on sale", but in the last 30 days it could be bought for less than now. Cashback and trade-in bonuses don't count – they only apply under conditions and may end early.
  • Crossed-out price not charged lately: the product never sold at the struck-through price while tracked, or last did more than 90 days ago.

Suspicious discounts appear in the list's "suspicious discount" filter, as an orange bar in the history and on the product page card.

Suspicious price

When Alza changes its website, the extension may read a wrong number, for example a monthly instalment instead of the price. Such a price is not hidden, so you don't miss a real discount. It is marked with and a dashed frame, and hovering over it shows why:

  • Less than half of the next price: the price is lower than half of the next higher price from the same check. It is left out of the minimum, the change arrow, the price rating and the suspicious discount.
  • Less than half of the usual price: the lowest price dropped below half of the usual price over the last 30 days. It counts normally, it is only marked. If the price stays, the warning disappears.

A change notification for such a product gets an extra warning line. Check the price on Alza.

The rating is based only on the history the extension recorded itself. It is not shown for newly tracked products (less than 14 days).

Target price

  1. Open the product's price history (or click " Target price" on the product page).
  2. Enter the price you want to buy at and click Watch.

As soon as the lowest price drops to the target or below, you get a notification – even if the page isn't open. It fires once; again only after the price rises above the target and drops again. A product with a target price is watched automatically.

Target per piece

For multipacks (capsules, nappies, pet food…) you can switch the field from Kč → Kč/ks (€/ks, Ft/db) and enter a price per piece, e.g. "2.40 Kč/ks". The extension converts it to the whole pack.

Background watching and notifications

  • Watched list: open the list and click Watch list in the extension popup, or click the bell on the list's tab. The whole list is watched; the list contents are refreshed before every background check, so items added elsewhere (for example in the Alza mobile app) are watched too and removed items are no longer checked. If the contents can't be loaded, the saved items are checked and the list updates the next time you open it.
  • Watched product: the " watch in background" button on a product page, or a target price.
  • The check runs every hour while the browser is running. In the settings you can change it to every 3, 6 or 12 hours or to once a day at a chosen time – if the browser is closed then, it runs a few minutes after it starts. The popup shows when the next one is due. Run it right away with Check now in the popup.
  • The number on the icon = products with a change since you last opened the popup, which lists the changes. Its colour: green = at least one product got cheaper, came back in stock, has a new sale or reached its target price, red = none got cheaper but at least one got more expensive, grey = only sales ended or unavailability. A blue number = old products suggested for deletion. An orange exclamation mark = the check stopped (you are not logged in to Alza, prices are shown without VAT, or Alza is blocking) and prices are not being collected – the reason and a login button are at the top of the popup. As soon as you log in to Alza, the check starts again by itself.
  • Changes in the popup: price drops, back in stock, a new sale and a reached target price in green, price increases in red (no notification for increases), end of sale and unavailability in grey. New changes have a coloured background, ones you've seen only coloured text. Watched lists show how many products got cheaper (▼) and more expensive (▲) over the last 7 days. Stop watching a list with Stop watching list in the popup on the open list, or in the settings (Watched lists tab). If you watch more than one Alza, lists, changes and watched products get a CZ / SK / HU label (hover it to see the Alza address). Help is in the settings ( bottom left).
  • Discount codes: codes from products you have viewed and your vouchers are in the settings, Codes and vouchers tab (more); the extension window shows a line with their number. When a watched product gets a new code, the notification includes the code.
  • Watch after purchase: the extension takes items bought in the last 14 days from “My orders” and watches their price. If it drops, you get “You bought it for X, now it costs Y. You can return it and buy it again at least until D.” Items bought online can be returned within 14 days of receipt – the extension counts cautiously from the order date, so the real deadline is usually a few days later. New orders are loaded when you visit Alza and during the background check; returned items are no longer watched. For a bought item you don't watch otherwise, you are only notified when it drops below the price you paid. In the popup's list of changes, a bought item you don't watch otherwise has an Orders link – it leads to My orders on Alza. Turn it off in the settings (Watch price after purchase); orders are read only in your browser.
  • Cashback reminder: when you buy an item that showed a cashback price on its product page, the extension reads the manufacturer’s registration deadline from the promotion page. It notifies you when the purchase can be registered (some promotions ask you to wait e.g. 30 days) and a week before the deadline; clicking opens the promotion terms. The “Cashback to register” list is in the extension window – tick it off with Done after registering. We read the deadline from the promotion text; if we can’t recognise it, the notification points you to the terms. You see the promotion dates – buy by and register by – before buying, too: in the tooltip of the cashback price in the list (with a link to the terms) and under the cashback row on the product page. Turn it off in the settings (Remind me to register cashback).

In the settings (Notifications tab) you choose what you want to hear about: price drops (optionally from a given percentage), new prices (sale, code, cashback), availability and quiet hours.

No notifications? In the settings click Test notifications. Below the button there's advice for your system (Windows / macOS) and, after the test, its result. If nothing arrives, check Windows "Do not disturb" / Focus assist and notification permission for your browser (Chrome, Firefox). On macOS, allow both Google Chrome and Google Chrome Helper (Alerts) in System Settings → Notifications (for Firefox, allow Firefox) and turn off Focus. In Safari, notifications are sent by the AllyZap app – allow AllyZap in Notifications. While the screen is shared or mirrored (e.g. remote access to the Mac), macOS hides notifications – at the top of Notifications, allow them when mirroring or sharing the display.

Real price per piece

Alza calculates the unit price only from the price for everyone – it ignores the AlzaPlus+ discount and discount codes. When stocking up you can't tell which pack is really the best deal. The extension works it out:

  • Categories and search: an orange label with the real price, e.g. 2,50 Kč/ks, sits before Alza's unit price. The “Unit price” tab with the extension icon next to Alza’s sort tabs loads the whole category and sorts it (click again to cancel). For drives, memory, USB flash drives and memory cards (per TB / GB), power banks (per 1000 mAh) and power stations (per Wh), where Alza shows no unit price, the extension calculates it itself – a grey label under the price, e.g. 1 995 Kč/TB – and sorts by it. For fridges, freezers, washing machines and dryers it shows the estimated electricity cost, e.g. running cost, 10 years: 8 400 Kč, and the “With running cost” tab sorts the listing by purchase price plus running cost; set the electricity price, the number of years and washes per week in the settings (Category listings → Running cost of appliances; the default 6 Kč, 0.20 € or 70 Ft per kWh is only a rough guide). On the product page the running cost is in the extension card – there for TVs, monitors and dishwashers too, which have no consumption data in listings. For TVs and monitors it counts with the number of hours a day they are on – set it in the same place (TV and monitor switched on … hours a day). TVs and monitors list their consumption only on the product page: above their listing a Calculate running cost button loads it (one page at a time, at most 48 at once) and saves it – the running cost label then shows in the listing and sorting. The extension also remembers the consumption from an opened product page.
  • Shopping list: price per piece for every price on hover, and "cheapest per piece" sorting.
  • Product page: a "per piece" tile with the price per piece for each price type. The pack size selector shows the price per piece (per metre for lengths) for every size in a column on the right – the best value in green, the most expensive in red. The closed selector shows the unit price of the chosen size right away too, including the best value / most expensive label.

The labels in categories can be turned off in the settings (Category listings → Real unit price).

My purchases and warranties

The extension can load everything you bought on Alza from the “My orders” archive. Turn it on in the settings with My purchases – purchased goods and warranties or with the button in the My purchases tab. Orders are read only in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

  • The My purchases tab in the settings: every purchase with its date, price per piece and in total, warranty status and a link to the order. Search by name, brand or category, a “warranty ends within 90 days” filter, a year and month selection with the sum of the selected purchases (clicking a chart column selects its year too; the months of that year then appear below it, with the same month a year earlier and the month-over-month and year-over-year change), and a chart of spending by year above the table (if you shop on several Alza sites, they are stacked within each year).
  • Warranty: the extension loads the warranty length from Alza together with your purchases (the value given for the product and valid for your account – with a business account the warranty for business purchases) and shows it in the table next to the end date (“36 mo.”). Where it cannot be determined, the end date is an estimate (purchase + 24 months) and the table says so. Whether a claim is still possible comes directly from Alza – with a longer warranty you will see “longer warranty – a claim is still possible”, returned and claimed goods are marked; returned goods do not count towards spending. Goods returned or claimed after they were loaded are recognised later: the extension reads the order again about a month after the purchase, while a claim is open and before a warranty reminder.
  • Remind me when a warranty ends: a month before the end you get a notification to check the item while you can still make a claim (during quiet hours it is postponed to their end). Only for goods from 1,000 Kč (40 €, 15,000 Ft) per piece.
  • Product detail: for goods you already own, the extension card shows “You bought it on…” with the date, the price and a link to the order.
  • Monthly budget: in the settings (Price tracking) enter how much you want to spend on your main Alza per month. The cart then shows how much you have already spent this month according to your orders, how much it will be with the cart and how much is left or by how much you exceed it.
  • Is AlzaPlus+ worth it? Above the table is an estimate for the last year: for purchases where the extension knows both the regular and the AlzaPlus+ price on the day of purchase from the history, it adds up the difference. From the prices in your orders it recognises whether you are a member (“you saved”) or not (“you would have saved”). Free delivery is not included; compare the membership fee yourself.
  • Invoice: the icon next to a purchase opens the invoice (PDF) from Alza – handy for a claim. The extension gets the link from the order only when you click (you must be signed in to Alza) and does not store it.
  • Running out? For goods you buy repeatedly and regularly (at least three times – capsules, pet food, filters…) the extension estimates when you will buy again. Above the chart is a list of what is running out, with today's price and whether it is below the usual price or worth waiting for a sale. On the product page the rhythm is in the “You bought…” note.
  • By category and savings: below the total of the selected purchases (period, search, filter) you see spending by Alza's main categories and how much you saved against the regular price on the day of purchase – thanks to sales, codes, AlzaPlus+ or benefits. Savings are counted only for purchases where the extension knows the regular price from the history.

The first load goes through your whole order history; prices and claim info are added gradually for purchases from the last two years. Turning the option off stops the loading and keeps the loaded purchases – delete them in the My purchases tab after turning the option off (Delete loaded purchases). “Delete all data” deletes them too; with the option on, however, the archive is loaded again at the next background check.

Codes and vouchers

Save discount codes and gift vouchers you received – an apology for a late order, a gift voucher, a prize – to the voucher wallet in the settings (Codes and vouchers tab). They stay in your browser only.

  • Adding: paste the text of the e-mail the voucher came in into the box at the top – the code, amount and expiry fill in by themselves (the order number is not taken as the code). Check it and click Add voucher. You can also fill it in by hand: code, value in Kč / € / Ft or in %, expiry, minimum order and a note.
  • In the cart: next to the “use a discount / gift voucher” link a bubble shows the vouchers you can use (on the right where there is room, otherwise below it). Apply applies a voucher; for a voucher with a minimum order you see how much is missing. Close the bubble with the cross – it stays hidden until the tab is closed.
  • Voucher from the cart: when you apply a voucher in the cart that is not in your wallet, the bubble offers Save to wallet – with the expiry and minimum shown by Alza.
  • A used voucher is recognised from your order (a voucher applied in the cart and a discount of the same value in a new order) and moved to used, with a link to the order. Mark it by hand with the Used button.
  • Reminder: a week before a voucher expires and on its last day you get a notification; click it to open the cart. Turn it off in the settings (Notifications → Remind me before vouchers expire).
  • Discount codes on products: under the wallet are the codes from products you have seen in the last 30 days (product page, list, background check) with the price with the code and a Copy button. When a watched product gets a new code, the notification includes the code. The extension window only shows a line with their number that opens the tab.

Settings and data

Open the page from the extension popup with the My data button or the gear (straight to the settings). The panel on the left has two groups: My data (Watched lists, Watched products, My purchases, Codes and vouchers, Stored data) and Settings (Price tracking, Notifications, Category listings, General – language, main Alza, backup, diagnostics – and Check log). What's new, Setup guide and Help are at the bottom of the panel.

  • Save prices while browsing (Price tracking) – prices of products you see in category listings, search results, product pages, carousels and on the home page are quietly saved to the history even if you don't watch them. When you come back to a product, you'll see how its price developed. Nothing is shown or notified. On by default.
  • Stored data – an overview of all products with a history and why they are stored ( list, product page, target price, browsing). Search, filters, price history via and deleting the data of a single product.
  • Backup (JSON) – saves the price history and stored prices, watched lists and products, target prices, cashback reminders, the voucher wallet including the codes, product notes, hidden products and brands, and settings to a file. A gift voucher is like cash – don't send a backup with vouchers to anyone. Purchases and product discount codes are not in the backup – they are loaded from Alza again. Restore from backup adds the data back (deletes nothing, history is merged).
  • History (CSV) – all prices for Excel.
  • Clean up old data – deletes products you don't watch that haven't been opened or checked for over 3 months (those saved while browsing only after a year). The extension suggests them itself with a blue number on the icon and a notice in the popup.
  • Delete all data – also deletes purchases, product discount codes, cashback reminders, the voucher wallet, notes, and hidden products and brands (settings and the error log stay); only after typing the word DELETE, a backup is downloaded automatically first. With price watching after purchase on, purchases are loaded again on your next visit to Alza; with My purchases on, the order archive is loaded again at the next background check.
  • Diagnostics – if something doesn't work, click Report a bug: a pre-filled report opens on GitHub (version, browser, recent errors – without product addresses, discount codes and names of purchased goods); you write the title yourself. The report is public, so check it before sending. Or download the diagnostics and send them to the author (version, settings, counts of stored data, the check log and errors – names of items you bought and order numbers are hidden; no passwords or cookies).

All data stays in your browser only. The extension talks only to Alza websites and sends nothing anywhere else. The only exception are the price comparison icons on the product page – they load directly from those websites, nothing else is sent to them.

FAQ

Prices are not saved / the check reports an error

Make sure you are logged in to Alza and "show prices without VAT" is off. Without logging in the extension deliberately saves nothing – the history would get mixed with another account's prices.

I don't see the price rating or the strikethrough check

They need at least 14 days of product history. Until then they stay hidden so they don't alarm you based on a few days of data.

Why doesn't the list reload after refreshing the page?

Prices loaded less than 15 minutes ago are taken from memory so the extension doesn't put needless load on Alza. After that they are loaded again.

Some products have no price per piece

The price per piece is shown only where the pack size can be determined reliably (the "number of pieces" parameter or a description like "360 pcs per pack").

I got a notification about a change but don't see it in the list

The background check saves prices to the history. Open the product's history or the list of changes in the popup – every captured change is there.

Moving to a new computer

Download a backup (JSON) on the old one and restore it in the settings on the new one. The history is merged with whatever is already there.

Why are there products I don't watch in Stored data?

They are products from shopping lists you opened and, with the “Save prices while browsing” option, products you saw while browsing Alza (). Their history comes in handy when you come back to the product. You can turn saving while browsing off in the settings; products you don't watch are suggested for deletion automatically (those saved while browsing only after a year).
